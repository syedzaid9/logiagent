# SOP-LOG-05: Detention, Demurrage, and Accessorial Billing

## 1. Free Time Allowance
- **Standard Live Load / Unload**: 2.0 hours of complimentary free time are provided at shipper origin and receiver destination.
- **Drop and Hook**: 30 minutes free time.
- Free time begins at the scheduled appointment time or actual arrival time, whichever is later, provided the driver checks in on time.

## 2. Detention Billing Rates
- Once free time expires, detention is billed in 15-minute increments at the standard rate of **$85.00 per hour** ($21.25 per 15 min).
- For specialized temperature-controlled Reefers, detention is billed at **$110.00 per hour** to account for secondary engine fuel burn and monitoring.
- Maximum allowable detention without advance dispatcher authorization is 4 hours. Beyond 4 hours, dispatcher may issue a truck order not used (TONU) or reposition the unit.

## 3. Layover and Truck Order Not Used (TONU)
- **TONU Fee**: Standard dry van TONU is $300.00 if canceled within 4 hours of dispatch. Reefer / specialized flatbed TONU is $450.00.
- **Layover Fee**: If a driver is held overnight due to shipper/receiver delay, layover is billed at $400.00/day plus verifiable hotel accommodations.

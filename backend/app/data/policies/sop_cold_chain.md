# SOP-LOG-03: Cold Chain Management and Temperature Compliance

## 1. Scope
Applies to all temperature-sensitive cargo (pharmaceuticals, vaccines, fresh produce, frozen proteins, and dairy) transported via refrigerated trailers (Reefers) and Sprinter temperature vans.

## 2. Temperature Ranges & Classifications
- **Deep Frozen (Cat A)**: -25°C to -18°C (-13°F to 0°F) for ice cream and specialized biologics.
- **Frozen (Cat B)**: -18°C to -12°C (0°F to 10°F) for frozen foods and meats.
- **Chilled (Cat C)**: +2°C to +8°C (36°F to 46°F) for pharmaceuticals, vaccines, dairy, and fresh produce.
- **Controlled Ambient (Cat D)**: +15°C to +25°C (59°F to 77°F) for medical devices and sensitive electronics.

## 3. Pre-Trip Inspection & Pre-Cooling
1. Reefer units must be pre-cooled to target set-point temperature at least 90 minutes prior to loading.
2. Drivers must verify that the temperature logger data-logger is active and recording in 5-minute intervals.
3. Cargo pulp temperatures must be probed and recorded on the Bill of Lading prior to signing acceptance.

## 4. Temperature Excursion Protocol
- A temperature excursion is defined as a deviation exceeding +/- 2.0°C from set-point lasting longer than 30 consecutive minutes.
- When an excursion alert triggers in LogiAgent:
  1. Driver must immediately pull over safely, check reefer error codes, and verify diesel fuel supply to the secondary generator unit.
  2. If reefer failure is mechanical, dispatch must route the vehicle to the nearest authorized Carrier Transicold / Thermo King repair facility or initiate emergency cross-dock transfer within 60 minutes.
  3. Quality Assurance must be notified within 20 minutes to assess product stability metrics.

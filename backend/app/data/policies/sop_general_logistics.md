# SOP-LOG-01: General Logistics Operations, Service Level Agreements (SLAs) and Proof of Delivery

## 1. On-Time Delivery SLA Requirements
- **Tier 1 Enterprise Customers**: 98.5% on-time delivery SLA measured against scheduled appointment windows (+/- 15 minutes).
- **Tier 2 Commercial Customers**: 95.0% on-time delivery SLA.
- Any shipment projected to arrive more than 30 minutes behind schedule must trigger an automated SLA Breach Warning via the Notification Tool.

## 2. Proof of Delivery (POD) Standards
- Electronic Proof of Delivery (e-POD) must be captured in the mobile driver application at destination.
- Required e-POD metadata:
  1. Recipient printed name and digital signature.
  2. Exact GPS geofence timestamp verifying truck presence within 150 meters of destination loading bay.
  3. Piece count verification and piece condition check.
  4. Photo of delivered pallet seals.
- Invoices cannot be submitted without an attached approved e-POD document.

## 3. Escalation Matrix for Logistics Emergencies
- **Level 1 (Minor delay < 60 min)**: Driver logs traffic update; automated ETA recalculation sent to customer portal.
- **Level 2 (Severe delay > 60 min or mechanical breakdown)**: Dispatcher contacted; nearest maintenance support unit dispatched or replacement vehicle requested via Vehicle Availability Tool.
- **Level 3 (Accident, cargo theft, major damage)**: Immediate notification to Risk Management, Insurance, Safety Officer, and Law Enforcement within 15 minutes.

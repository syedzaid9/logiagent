# SOP-LOG-02: Failed Delivery and Exception Handling Protocol

## 1. Purpose and Scope
This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) outlines the mandatory steps required when a commercial or residential shipment cannot be successfully completed at the designated destination. This applies to all Logistics Coordinators, Dispatchers, and Drivers operating under LogiAgent management.

## 2. Definition of Delivery Failure
A delivery is classified as "Failed" when any of the following conditions occur:
- Recipient is unavailable or unreachable after three (3) contact attempts within 30 minutes.
- Delivery location is closed, inaccessible, or refuses to accept cargo.
- Delivery documentation (e.g., Bill of Lading, Proof of Delivery) is refused or disputed.
- Severe cargo damage or seal tampering is identified prior to unloading.

## 3. Immediate Driver Actions
When a delivery failure occurs:
1. **Wait Time**: The driver must hold at the location for a mandatory 15-minute grace period before departing.
2. **Contact Protocol**: Dispatcher must attempt immediate contact with the receiver and the shipping customer via the primary emergency contact numbers.
3. **Photographic Evidence**: The driver must take two (2) clear photographs of the delivery site showing the vehicle, the closed facility/door, and timestamp proof through the mobile driver app.
4. **Exception Logging**: The driver logs exception code `EXC-FAIL-01` into LogiAgent.

## 4. Re-delivery and Redirection Policy
- **First Re-delivery Attempt**: Authorized within 24 hours at standard contract rates if the customer confirms receipt capability.
- **Redirection**: If redirected to an alternate hub within a 50 km radius, a standard redirection surcharge of $150.00 plus $2.20 per additional kilometer will apply.
- **Secondary Re-delivery Failure**: Cargo must be returned to the nearest regional distribution hub (Distribution Center) for secure storage.

## 5. Storage and Detention Surcharges
- Complimentary storage is granted for the first 24 hours following return to the hub.
- Commencing at hour 25, storage is billed at $45.00 per pallet/day for standard dry goods, and $95.00 per pallet/day for temperature-controlled reefer cargo.
- Maximum holding period is fourteen (14) calendar days before cargo is declared abandoned pursuant to Section 7 of the Uniform Commercial Code (UCC).

## 6. Customer Notification and Escalation
The Operations Team must issue an automated Exception Incident Report via email and SMS to the customer within 45 minutes of failure declaration.

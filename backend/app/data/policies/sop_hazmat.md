# SOP-LOG-06: Hazardous Materials (HAZMAT) Transport Protocol

## 1. Regulatory Authority
Transport of hazardous materials adheres to 49 CFR Parts 100-185 and DOT Hazardous Materials Regulations (HMR).

## 2. Driver and Equipment Certification
- Only drivers possessing active **CDL-A with Hazardous Materials (H) or Tanker/HAZMAT (X) endorsement** and valid TSA threat assessment clearance may be assigned to HAZMAT loads.
- The vehicle must carry 4 DOT-compliant reflective placards corresponding to the UN chemical classification code.
- A fully certified 10-B:C dry chemical fire extinguisher, spill containment kit, and Emergency Response Guidebook (ERG) must be onboard.

## 3. Shipping Papers & Emergency Contact
- Hazardous materials shipping papers must be within immediate reach of the driver when restrained by the seatbelt, or stored in the driver's door pouch.
- A 24-hour monitored CHEMTREC / emergency response telephone number must be printed on the Bill of Lading.

## 4. Prohibited Tunnel and Route Restrictions
- HAZMAT loads are prohibited from traversing designated metropolitan tunnels and non-truck bypass arteries. Route Optimization Tool must apply `HAZMAT_RESTRICTED` routing flags to bypass prohibited corridors.
